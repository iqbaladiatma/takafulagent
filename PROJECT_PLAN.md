# Project Plan for UJIKOM RPL 2025

## Problem Statement
Takaful Keluarga is a business engaged in Sharia Insurance and needs good marketing to make more customers interested in their protection products. Moreover, there are already many businesses engaged in the financial sector that are developing rapidly and are known by the wider community. So, Takaful Keluarga took the initiative to promote their business through a website to be more effective and efficient in reaching potential customers and supporting their agents. The primary focus of this project is to provide a comprehensive directory of professional agents to help customers find the right protection advisor.

## Research Question
1. How can users be interested and trust to buy the insurance products?
2. How can we promote the products to all users effectively?
3. How can users easily find and contact professional agents?

## Based on your knowledge and explorations, tell us the Website Development Part of your project?
In front-end aspects, we created this project with **Laravel Blade** as our templating engine. In styling aspects, we use **Tailwind CSS** framework, and also we use **Alpine.js** to make dynamic and interactive websites (e.g., mobile menu, animations). We prioritize a responsive and clean design to build trust with customers.

## Based on your knowledge and explorations, tell us the Web/Back End Part of your project?
In back-end aspects, availability for the agents/admins to have authentications is supported using **MySQL** as the data container. We use **Laravel 12** framework for the core logic. For the Admin Panel, we utilize **Filament PHP** (v3.2) which provides a powerful and rapid development environment for managing agents, users, and content. We do not use a separate API for the frontend but integrate directly via Blade views and Controllers. **We do not handle payment processing or checkout flows.**
